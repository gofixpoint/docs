Use Amika from the surface that fits the task. You can send work to an agent,
connect directly to a sandbox, or open the sandbox in your editor.

## Work with agents

Send a prompt to an agent when you want Amika to provision a sandbox and run
the task for you. You can also review and continue the resulting session.
